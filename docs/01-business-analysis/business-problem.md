# Business Problem

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** AI-Powered OJT Management & Risk Prediction System

The system is designed to support universities in managing the entire On-the-Job Training (OJT) lifecycle of students.

The platform centralizes OJT information, tracks student progress, identifies students who may be at risk of OJT delay, and supports lecturers and OJT coordinators in making early interventions.

---

## 2. Business Context

Universities may need to manage hundreds or thousands of students participating in OJT programs every semester.

OJT-related information may be distributed across multiple sources such as:

- Excel spreadsheets
- Email
- Online forms
- Student information systems
- Lecturer reports
- Company feedback
- Manual documents

Because the information is fragmented, OJT coordinators and lecturers may have difficulty obtaining a complete and up-to-date view of each student's OJT status.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

The current OJT management process is primarily reactive.

Students who are experiencing difficulties may only be identified after significant delays or problems have already occurred.

Examples include:

- Student has not found an OJT company.
- Student has not completed required academic credits.
- Student has not submitted an OJT plan.
- Student misses important milestones.
- Student progress is significantly behind schedule.
- Student is approaching an OJT deadline.
- Lecturer or coordinator detects the problem too late.

As a result, intervention may occur only after the student's probability of successful on-time completion has already decreased.

---

## 4. Root Causes

### 4.1 Fragmented Data

OJT data may be stored across multiple systems and files.

### 4.2 Manual Monitoring

Lecturers and coordinators may need to manually review student information to identify problems.

### 4.3 Lack of Early Warning

Existing processes may not provide an automatic mechanism for identifying students with increasing OJT risk.

### 4.4 Limited Progress Visibility

Stakeholders may not have a centralized dashboard showing the current progress of all students.

### 4.5 Delayed Intervention

Without early risk detection, corrective actions may only be taken after major problems appear.

---

## 5. Business Impact

These problems may lead to:

- OJT completion delays
- Increased workload for lecturers and coordinators
- Poor visibility of student progress
- Late intervention
- Inefficient communication
- Inconsistent OJT monitoring
- Difficulty identifying high-risk students
- Reduced ability to make data-driven decisions

---

## 6. Proposed Solution

Develop a centralized OJT Management Platform that provides:

1. Student management
2. OJT planning
3. OJT progress tracking
4. Milestone management
5. Centralized dashboards
6. AI-based OJT delay risk prediction
7. Risk explanation
8. Recommended intervention actions
9. Intervention tracking
10. Reporting and analytics

---

## 7. Business Transformation

The system aims to transform the OJT management process from:

**Reactive Management**

> A student has already experienced OJT delay, then the university responds.

into:

**Proactive Management**

> The system identifies increasing risk early, allowing lecturers and coordinators to intervene before serious delay occurs.

---

## 8. Business Goals

### BG-01
Centralize OJT information into a single management platform.

### BG-02
Provide timely visibility into student OJT progress.

### BG-03
Identify students who are at risk of OJT delay.

### BG-04
Enable earlier intervention by lecturers and OJT coordinators.

### BG-05
Provide explainable risk information instead of only displaying a prediction.

### BG-06
Support data-driven OJT management decisions.

### BG-07
Reduce manual monitoring workload.

### BG-08
Create a traceable history of student progress and intervention activities.

---

## 9. Preliminary Success Metrics

The following metrics may later be used to evaluate the effectiveness of the system:

- Percentage of active OJT students monitored centrally
- Number of students identified as high risk before a confirmed delay occurs
- Average time between risk detection and intervention
- OJT milestone completion rate
- OJT completion rate
- OJT delay rate
- Number of manual monitoring activities required
- AI model performance
- Lecturer/coordinator response time

Exact KPI targets will be defined after baseline data is available.
