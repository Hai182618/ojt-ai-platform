# Success Metrics

## 1. Purpose

Define measurable indicators that can later be used to evaluate whether the system achieves its business goals.

No unsupported improvement percentage is assumed at this stage.

---

## 2. Operational Metrics

### KPI-01 — Centralized Monitoring Coverage
**Definition:** Percentage of active OJT students represented in the platform with current status data.

### KPI-02 — Milestone Completion Rate
**Definition:** Percentage of required milestones completed by their expected due dates.

### KPI-03 — OJT Delay Rate
**Definition:** Percentage of OJT students whose completion exceeds the expected OJT schedule.

### KPI-04 — Intervention Response Time
**Definition:** Average time between high-risk identification and first recorded intervention.

### KPI-05 — Open High-Risk Cases
**Definition:** Number of high-risk students without an active or completed intervention.

### KPI-06 — Manual Monitoring Effort
**Definition:** Estimated number or duration of manual activities required to review student progress.

---

## 3. AI Metrics

### KPI-07 — Recall for Delayed Students
Measures how many truly delayed students are identified by the model.

### KPI-08 — Precision for High-Risk Predictions
Measures how many students classified as high risk actually correspond to observed delay outcomes.

### KPI-09 — F1 Score
Provides a balance between precision and recall.

### KPI-10 — ROC-AUC / PR-AUC
Used where appropriate to evaluate ranking quality across thresholds.

### KPI-11 — Prediction Coverage
Percentage of eligible students for whom the system can generate a valid prediction.

### KPI-12 — Missing-Input Rate
Percentage of attempted predictions that cannot be completed due to missing required inputs.

---

## 4. Adoption and Usability Metrics

### KPI-13 — Active User Rate
Percentage of expected users who actively use the system during an OJT period.

### KPI-14 — Task Completion Rate
Percentage of users who successfully complete key workflows such as OJT plan submission or intervention creation.

### KPI-15 — Data Completeness
Percentage of required OJT records with all mandatory fields completed.

---

## 5. KPI Target Policy

Numeric targets will only be set after:

1. baseline data is available;
2. the measurement period is defined;
3. the stakeholder owner agrees on the KPI;
4. the metric can be measured reliably.

This prevents unsupported claims such as "reduce delays by 30%" before evidence exists.
