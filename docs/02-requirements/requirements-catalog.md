## Priority Definitions

- **Must** — Required for the intended MVP business flow.
- **Should** — Important but can be delivered after the core end-to-end flow if needed.
- **Could** — Valuable enhancement, not required for MVP.
- **Won't (Now)** — Explicitly excluded from the current release.
